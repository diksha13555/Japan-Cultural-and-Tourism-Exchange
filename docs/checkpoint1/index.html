The problem問題
01 · The problem

Getting to Japan is easy.
Being in Japan isn't.

We followed one traveller from her seat on the plane to her first hour in Japan.

1

The flight

Booking, boarding and a window seat above the clouds. As simple as it sounded.

2

Landing

The billboards say a lot, but nothing she can understand. No plan and no context.

3

Asking for help

A language mismatch. Every attempt to get help adds a new problem to her list.

4

Missing out

She sees the tourist surface, but never the culture she imagined.

What we found課題
01 · Root causes

Two problems at the core

🗓️

Planning overload

Opening hours, travel times, bookings, budgets and diet are spread across blogs, maps, booking sites and spreadsheets. One delay breaks the whole day.

⛩️

Cultural disconnect

Etiquette and traditions are invisible to outsiders. Authentic local shops, homestays and festivals are hard to find, so visitors leave without really meeting Japan.

…and they make each other worse:

🈳 Language barrier🚉 Complex transit🆘 Safety in emergencies♿ One-size-fits-all apps💸 Budget surprises

Our idea解決策
02 · The idea

A guide, not a map

It starts by asking what you want to do, then handles the rest: where to go, how to get there, what it means, how to book it and how to stay safe.

Open the app Language & UI style "What do you want to do?" Eat · Go · Relax · Meet · Explore Route + itinerary + booking Pay in-app, save more
🗺️

AI itinerary

A full day in a few taps. Change anything along the way, book directly and use it offline.

📷

See & hear

Point at a sign or menu and hear it in your language through your earphones.

🍜

Smart discovery

Food by taste, diet and budget; quiet spots; live events near you.

🎎

Culture companion

Etiquette on the spot, local homestays, authentic crafts, and Japanese in small daily lessons.

🤝

Local partners

Cafés, stays, shops and guides, with in-app deals for travellers.

🆘

SOS, always free

Share your location, call emergency services, use translated medical phrases and get disaster alerts.
