# 00 · Story & Positioning (everyone reads this)

## One-liner
- **"Trip DNA makes Japan easy, so you spend your time living its culture, not managing your trip."**
- Short: **Less worry. More culture.**

## Problem (Tourism & Cultural Exchange track, Hakuhodo)
- **Planning overload:** a "perfect" plan collapses with one delay; it's spread across 7+ apps.
- **Cultural disconnect:** language, invisible etiquette and hidden local places, so visitors see the surface and leave without *meeting* Japan.
- **Insight:** **stress kills curiosity.** Anxious travellers retreat to safe hotspots, so culture loses.

## How we PROMOTE tourism & cultural exchange (judge feedback #2)
Say it in this order: **free the time → aim it at culture → make it two-way → measure it.**
1. **Free the time:** stress-test + auto-repair remove planning panic; the hours saved go back to the trip.
2. **Aim it at culture:** every itinerary has at least one local slot (tea ceremony, craft workshop, matsuri, homestay dinner), plus etiquette at every stop.
3. **Make it two-way:**
   - Locals **host**: homestays, workshops, small shops and guides earn from visitors.
   - Visitors **give back**: language-exchange meetups; sharing their own culture with hosts (e.g. an Indian cooking night at a homestay).
   - **Culture Passport:** stamps for respectful local experiences.
4. **Spread tourism:** suggest local neighbourhoods beyond overcrowded hotspots. This helps small businesses and eases overtourism.
5. **Measure it:** culture hours per trip · ¥ spent at local small businesses · % of visits outside the top-20 hotspots · passport stamps.

## USP: answer to "why would anyone use this?"
- **Tests your plan before reality does:** failure injection → domino map → minimal-change repair. Nobody else does this.
- **All-in-one:** plan, navigate, book, culture and SOS in one guide.
- **For everyone:** Vibrant / Simple / Colourblind-safe UI · 5 languages · student and disability discounts.
- **Trustworthy AI:** it recommends only verified places.

## Users (priority)
1. Leisure tourists from India, China and Korea · 2. Students · 3. Business travellers

## Hakuhodo angle (creative/communication agency)
- Lead with the **human story** and the **insight** ("stress kills curiosity").
- Show how partners and brands **reach** travellers: airlines, station QR codes, in-app deals.

## Words
- Use: "experience Japan", "local hosts", "exchange", "live the moment".
- Avoid: "tourist trap", "cheap", "perfect trip".
