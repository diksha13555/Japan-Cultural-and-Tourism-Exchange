# Trip DNA · Less worry. More culture.
**Team Out of Bounds** (Madhav Arora, Diksha Mahajan, Paridhi Thareja, Bhagya Vardhan)
Thapar Japan Hackathon · Tourism & Cultural Exchange · problem statement by Hakuhodo

Trip DNA is a travel guide for Japan. It plans your day, stress-tests it, repairs it when reality breaks it, and spends the time it saves on real local culture: tea with a host, a craft, a festival.

| Path | What it is |
|---|---|
| `/` | Hub page linking every part |
| `/deck/` | The combined pitch deck (起承転結: problem → product → business → close). Use → to present |
| `/prototype/` | Live prototype: plan a Tokyo day, repair a missed train, switch Leisure/Business/Study, Tabi Feed. `#click` = presenter mode |
| `/film/` | The short film: her first day in Japan, from grey to colour |
| `/business/` | Research, market sizing, 16 competitors, business model, unit economics, impact metrics, survey (simulated, labelled) |
| `/finale/` | Arigatō finale slides + Q&A drawer |
| `/docs/` | Plan, story, interviews, checkpoint 1 deck |

Everything is static HTML/CSS/JS and runs offline. All places, prices and survey figures are **sample or simulated** and labelled as such. Revenue figures come from our model (`business/model.py`).

Refresh this folder from the working project with `sync.ps1`. Deploying: see `git_instructions.md`.
