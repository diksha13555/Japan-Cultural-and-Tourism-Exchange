# Impact Metrics: how Trip DNA *proves* it promotes tourism and cultural exchange
**Answers judge feedback #2.** Built with WS2: the Culture Passport counters in the app are these metrics.
Tags [S#] = sources in `research.md`; [A#] = assumption. Targets are illustrative pilot goals (labelled).

---

## 1. The logic (say it in this order on stage)
**Free the time → aim it at culture → make it two-way → spread it out → measure it.**

| Step | What the app does | Output | Outcome | Impact |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Free the time | stress-test + one-tap repair | planning minutes saved | less stress | curiosity instead of panic |
| Aim it at culture | ≥ 1 local slot per day + etiquette at every stop | culture hours booked | more real experiences | visitors understand Japan |
| Make it two-way | host *exchange offers*, meetups | exchange sessions | locals meet the world | cultural exchange, not consumption |
| Spread it out | "nearby, more local" suggestions | visits outside hotspots | less crowding | overtourism eased, regions earn |
| Measure it | Culture Passport + partner dashboard | the 6 metrics below | — | reportable to JTA/DMOs and sponsors |

---

## 2. The six metrics

| # | Metric | Definition / formula | Captured in app by | Baseline (sourced) | Pilot target [A] |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Culture hours per trip** | Σ duration of culture stops + host bookings marked "I did this" | culture card ✓ + booking check-in | experiences are only **4.5%** of inbound spend (¥0.42T of ¥9.46T) [S4] | **≥ 3 h per trip-day planned**; ≥ 6 h per trip |
| **2** | **¥ to local small businesses** | GMV paid to independent hosts / SMEs (not chains) | booking records, partner type flag | food + experiences = ¥2.49T/yr total [S4]; the small-business share is not measured anywhere (gap) | ≥ 60% of in-app GMV goes to independent hosts |
| **3** | **% of visits outside the top-20 hotspots** | stops not in our top-20 list ÷ all stops | itinerary + check-ins vs `hotspots.json` | **69.4%** of foreign guests stay in the 3 big metro areas (2024), up from 62.7% in 2019 [S11] | ≥ 40% of stops outside top-20 |
| **4** | **Host earnings** | Σ (booking value × (1 − commission)) per host per month | partner dashboard | hosts keep 65–80% on Klook/GYG (15–35% fees) [C6, C7] | hosts keep **88%**; +¥800–2,300 more per ¥10,000 booking |
| **5** | **Passport stamps** | stamps per user per trip (respectful local experiences done) | Culture Passport | none: no app measures this (our gap) | ≥ 4 stamps per trip |
| **6** | **Two-way exchange sessions** | sessions where the visitor shared their culture (host-confirmed) | host taps "exchange happened"; meetup check-in | none (our gap) | ≥ 1 in 3 host bookings include an exchange |

Supporting: planning minutes saved per repair (stress-test log) · % disrupted plans repaired with must-dos kept · NPS · host rating of guest etiquette.

---

## 3. Why these metrics matter to the people in the room
- **Japan Tourism Agency / government:** the 2026–30 plan targets **130M regional overnight stays**, **100 regions** with overtourism measures, **¥250k spend per visitor** and **40M repeaters** by 2030 [S9]. Metrics 2, 3 and 6 report directly on those goals.
- **Hakuhodo (the problem statement owner):** culture hours and exchange sessions give brands a *new, human KPI* to sponsor ("this campaign created 12,000 culture hours").
- **Hosts:** metric 4 is their pay cheque, and metric 6 is why they enjoy hosting.
- **UN SDG 8.9:** sustainable tourism that "creates jobs and promotes local culture and products". Our metrics are a direct proxy.

## 5. Honesty rules
- Pilot targets are **goals, not results**. Label them "Pilot target".
- Demo counters are **sample data**. Label them "Sample".
- Baselines come only from the sources above. Where nothing exists (metrics 5 and 6) we say "no one measures this today", and that is the point.
